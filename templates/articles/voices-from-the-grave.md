# Voices from the Grave

Have you ever been haunted? Have you had an experience that left a memory so clear you seem to relive it long after it is over? Perhaps a parent, another relative, or a former partner ruled the way you thought, acted, and believed. You may still be living by their rules without even realizing it. Those rules can cause pain in your life and in the lives of people you love and are responsible for. Al-Anon gives you tools to recognize how your life has been haunted.

## The voice you are still trying to please

These hauntings can take different forms. You may still relive painful experiences with an alcoholic parent. Or the voice may belong to someone who never drank but controlled your life so completely that you still struggle to make decisions of your own. That person may have died, may be out of your life, or may still be part of it. The old rules can keep their hold in any of those circumstances. It is those rules and their authority over your life that need to be laid to rest.

A belief or rule can become ingrained in you, controlling the choices you make years after you learned it. That is a voice from the grave you are still trying to please. It can rob you of serenity and stand between you and your Higher Power.

> A voice from the grave may be one you are still trying to please.

The rules you grew up with can shape how you try to earn love and approval in adult relationships. You may find yourself trying to rescue or save someone, or falling into the habit of mothering, managing and manipulating them. So much of your attention goes toward pleasing the other person that you lose sight of what you want. Over time, you can lose your sense of who you are apart from that relationship.

Al-Anon can help you see the connection between those old rules and the way you relate to a husband, wife, or someone else you love today. You begin to recognize when you are making choices to earn their approval and how much of your own life you have set aside in the process. Seeing that pattern is the beginning of developing a life of your own.

The Al-Anon program is gracefully simple and patient. It is a program of recovery that changes the way you live your life and the way you think about your life. As you work the Steps, changes can take place so gradually that you may not notice them until you awaken one day to find that a part of your past no longer haunts you. If you are willing to commit to practicing the Al-Anon program, change can happen.

## Put them right back in the grave

This is where the program helps you silence the voices from the grave that keep you stuck. When an old voice returns and begins to rule how you feel or act, recognize that familiar danger zone. A judgment learned years ago is reaching into the life you are living today.

Putting those voices back in the grave means letting go of their authority over your life. One way to quiet them is to find your own voice. You can acknowledge the hurt and ask God to help you speak with confidence about how you want to live today. When an old voice starts telling you what to do, you can remind yourself:

“I have lived by your rules long enough. With God’s help, I am learning to trust my own voice and make my own choices.”

The memory may take time to lose its hold. As you grow in the program, those voices can grow quieter. When one returns during the day, you can recognize it and put it back in the past where it belongs. “One day at a time” is how you practice living in the present.

## Let go and let God

In Al-Anon, the Twelve Steps are key to letting go of a past that keeps you from living a life of your own. They help you get to know yourself without depending on approval from the voices that haunt you. With the program’s tools and support, you can begin to make decisions for your own life and develop a relationship with the God of your understanding.

The key to living a life of your own, free from the rule of old pain and anger, is in your relationship with your Higher Power and your willingness to maintain that relationship.

> The voices from the past can make it difficult to hear your Higher Power’s voice in the present.

It is a matter of willingness and turning your problems over to God. It is also a willingness to listen to and talk with God, seeking the direction and serenity that come from that relationship. Why keep giving those old voices the power to rule your life today? The goal is to “Let go and let God.”

## Meetings, a sponsor, and your “God squad”

Let the God of your understanding lift you up and sustain you every day. Al-Anon helps you do that through meetings, where you listen to other members’ experiences and find something you recognize in your own life.

> Let the God of your understanding lift you up and sustain you every day.

You can ask someone with experience in the program to be your sponsor: someone who can take you through the Twelve Steps, listen to you, and help you develop your understanding. The Steps give you a solid foundation for your life and your relationship with your Higher Power.

It also helps to have other people you can call. Some people call this their “God squad”: members they have come to know in meetings and can turn to for help, guidance, and encouragement. If your sponsor is unavailable, you have others to reach out to.

### When the past starts to haunt you

**Recognize the voice.** Whose approval am I still trying to earn?

**Put its authority back in the past.** That person’s judgment does not have to rule your life today.

**Find your own voice.** Learning to trust my own voice.

**Let go and let God.** Turn the hurt, fear, or anger over to the God of your understanding.

**Reach out.** Let the encouragement of others help you find your way.

Practice this one day at a time. Each time you recognize an old voice, you can turn back toward the life you are living today.

## A loving map of daily living

The Al-Anon program is a loving map of daily living. Along with the Steps and the help of others, it offers a way toward peace and a clearer view of your own life. As [*From Survival to Recovery*](https://ecomm.al-anon.org/ItemDetail?iProductCode=B21) expresses it: “Our sight, once clouded and confused, will clear and we will be able to perceive reality and recognize truth.”

This is a path to peace.
