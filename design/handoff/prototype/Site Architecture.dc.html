Individual readings
All 366 use the home template. Reached from another reading’s “Keep reading”, from the calendar, from Most read, and from every topic and Step list — never from a nav item.
Depth
Nothing sits more than three clicks from the header. A reading is one click from Topics via a collection, or one from Most read.
Return paths
A reading ends in its own topic, its Step, two related topics, and three sibling readings. Step pages point back at topics; background topics point at the collections that carry the subject.
The one gap
The calendar. Two live pages already link to it, and without it 365 of the 366 readings have no crawlable route.
