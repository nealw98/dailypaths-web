Courage to Change

One Day at a Time in Al-Anon II

B-16 · 384 pages · 1992 · $16.00

The book you’ll see on the table at almost every Al-Anon meeting. For many, it’s the first piece of recovery literature they ever hold — and the one they keep coming back to. Each day offers a short, honest reflection from a real Al-Anon member about what it’s like to love someone who drinks, and the small shifts that start to change everything. Open to today’s date. That’s all you need to do.
