How Al-Anon Works

For Families & Friends of Alcoholics

B-32 · 416 pages · 2008 · $9.00

Al-Anon’s “big book.” If someone asks you what Al-Anon is, this is the book you hand them. It covers everything — what the program is, how meetings work, what the Steps mean in practice — all through the shared experience of real members. It’s the most comprehensive single introduction to Al-Anon there is, and often the book that helps people finally understand they belong here.
