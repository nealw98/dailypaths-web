August: Willingness

Becoming willing to make things right.

Looking at harm requires honesty about our own actions, even when other people have hurt us too. It also takes care: accepting responsibility does not mean taking blame for everything. These readings explore willingness to repair relationships while keeping a clear view of what is ours to address.

A small practice Consider one relationship and write down the specific actions you regret. Separate your actions from the other person’s choices.

Step 8

Made a list of all persons we had harmed, and became willing to make amends to them all.

A question to carry

What is mine to acknowledge in this relationship?
