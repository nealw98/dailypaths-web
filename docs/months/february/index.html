February: Hope

Opening to the possibility that help is available.

Hope may arrive before certainty. It can begin with hearing someone describe a familiar problem and a different way of living with it. This month’s readings explore being willing to receive help, question old assumptions, and consider a Higher Power without having every answer settled.

A small practice Listen for one useful idea in a meeting or conversation. Try it in an ordinary situation and notice what changes.

Step 2

Came to believe that a Power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity.

A question to carry

Where could I accept help instead of insisting I must figure everything out alone?
