March: Faith

Choosing trust over self-will.

Turning something over does not mean abandoning your life. It can mean taking the next responsible action without trying to guarantee the result. These readings explore the daily choice to seek guidance, loosen your grip, and act from something steadier than fear.

A small practice Name one worry, ask for guidance, and identify one action that is yours to take. Leave space for an outcome you cannot arrange.

Step 3

Made a decision to turn our will and our lives over to the care of God as we understood Him.

A question to carry

What would I do next if I did not have to control how this turns out?
