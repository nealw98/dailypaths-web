December: Service

Carrying recovery forward through service.

Recovery becomes visible in how we live and how we treat people. Sharing our experience can help someone else, and practicing these principles in ordinary relationships keeps us learning. These readings explore service, connection, and giving freely without taking responsibility for another person’s recovery.

A small practice Offer one small act of service without managing the response. Notice the difference between being available and making yourself indispensable.

Step 12

Having had a spiritual awakening as the result of these steps, we tried to carry this message to others, and to practice these principles in all our affairs.

A question to carry

How can I share what has helped me while leaving another person free to find their own way?
