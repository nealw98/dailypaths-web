June: Patience

Allowing change to unfold in its own time.

Recognizing a habit does not always make us ready to release it. Rescuing, controlling, or seeking approval may have helped us feel safer for a long time. This month’s readings explore willingness: seeing the cost of a familiar response and becoming open to another way.

A small practice Notice the moment before you repeat a familiar reaction. Pause long enough to ask whether you are willing to try something different.

Step 6

Were entirely ready to have God remove all these defects of character.

A question to carry

What familiar response am I still protecting, even though it keeps hurting me?
