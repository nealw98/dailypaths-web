November: Spiritual Awareness

Deepening conscious contact through prayer and meditation.

A little quiet can reveal how much noise we have been living inside. Prayer and meditation offer time to listen, ask for guidance, and return our attention to the present. These readings explore a spiritual practice that can fit into a real day, including days when stillness feels difficult.

A small practice Set aside a few quiet minutes. Let one concern come into view, ask for guidance, and return gently when your attention wanders.

Step 11

Sought through prayer and meditation to improve our conscious contact with God as we understood Him, praying only for knowledge of His will for us and the power to carry that out.

A question to carry

What am I hearing when I give myself a little room to listen?
