Step Four: Courage

Made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves.

Having established a solid spiritual foundation in the first three acceptance Steps—admitting our powerlessness, opening our minds to hope, and deciding to trust a Power greater than ourselves—we arrive at Step Four. Step Four challenges us to take direct action through nine simple words: "Made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves".
