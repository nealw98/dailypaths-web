September: Brotherly Love

Making amends with care, compassion, and love.

Repair asks more of us than finding the right words. It may involve an apology, changed behavior, repayment, or respecting a boundary. These readings explore making amends with care for the people affected, rather than simply seeking relief from our own discomfort.

A small practice Before approaching someone, discuss the situation with your sponsor. Consider whether contact would help or cause further harm, and what changed behavior you can begin now.

Step 9

Made direct amends to such people wherever possible, except when to do so would injure them or others.

A question to carry

What would meaningful repair look like from the other person’s side?
