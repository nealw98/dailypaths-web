January: Acceptance

Accepting what you cannot control.

So much energy can go into watching, persuading, and preparing for someone else’s drinking that our own lives become an afterthought. Acceptance begins with noticing what our efforts can and cannot change. These readings return to the relief—and discomfort—of putting down responsibility that was never ours to carry.

A small practice Notice one situation you are trying to manage for another adult. What part belongs to you, and what can you leave with them?

Step 1

We admitted we were powerless over alcohol — that our lives had become unmanageable.

A question to carry

What is my effort to control this costing me today?
