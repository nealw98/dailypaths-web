May: Honesty

Telling the truth and releasing what was hidden.

Some things grow heavier when we keep them to ourselves. Sharing honestly with a trusted person can help us see our experience more clearly and feel less alone. These readings explore the difference between hiding, explaining everything away, and allowing another person to know us.

A small practice Choose one thing you have been avoiding saying to a trusted sponsor or support person. Begin with the plainest description you can give.

Step 5

Admitted to God, to ourselves, and to another human being the exact nature of our wrongs.

A question to carry

What am I afraid someone would think if I told the truth about this?
