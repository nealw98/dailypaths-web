The Twelve Concepts of Service

The Concepts of Service describe how responsibility and authority are shared throughout Al-Anon’s service structure. They address participation, delegation, clear roles, and the importance of hearing minority voices. We can apply these principles in our own recovery by sharing responsibility without taking over, trusting others to do their part, and listening when someone sees things differently.
