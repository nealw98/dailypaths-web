## Editorial assessments

- **Letting Go:** complete and focused on worry, with a recognizable night-shift opening. Its value differs from the guides' practical instruction. No body rewrite needed. Approved diagrams and text alternatives remain; only the deferred One Day at a Time promotion is suppressed.
- **The Line I Kept Moving:** a complete personal narrative whose changing boundary, dinner scene, and recognition of a recurring pattern carry the point. Do not recast it as a how-to guide. The deferred Finding Yourself card and unwritten Power of Saying No card are suppressed; other live recommendations remain.
- **Voices from the Grave:** a developed educational article about internalized authority rather than a chronological personal account. It has its own metaphor, three pull quotes, and five-action insert. Preserve the supplied spiritual language and ending. CMS catalog credits Jeff J.; FOUNDATION still says attribution is unconfirmed. No credit is invented here.
- **Detachment:** the new draft replaces an inherited general essay with everyday decisions about monitoring, rescuing, covering up, managing tasks, and following someone else's mood. It includes bounded help, shared responsibilities, disability/care exceptions, safe communication, and emergency distinctions. It avoids repeating the Three Cs or the Surrender guide's full control framework.
- **Finding Help:** the existing introduction had absolute privacy/payment assurances and a narrow Alateen age assertion. The draft replaces those with a grounded introduction and direct official resources. The first-meeting article carries the practical detail so the guide can stay a manageable doorway.
- **First meeting:** no standalone article was found in the repository, handoffs, or eleven-page published catalog. /start/ contains a short orientation. The new article is explicitly editorial, with no manufactured member experience.
- **Other topics:** Focus on Yourself, Trusting a Higher Power, Understanding the Disease, and Community & Fellowship remain supporting inherited topic pages. The twelve Step articles also remain. None is newly declared an approved launch guide. No additional guide was removed.
- **No filler:** the only added article fills the explicit newcomer orientation gap. No count target or speculative contributor titles were used.
